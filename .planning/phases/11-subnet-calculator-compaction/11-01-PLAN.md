## SUBNET-09 no-scroll target (stated before implementation)

**Target:** at a 1366x650 viewport, loading `/tools/subnet?cidr=2001%3Adb8%3A%3A%2F64` (and the long-value sample `/tools/subnet?cidr=fd12%3A3456%3A789a%3Abcde%3A%3A%2F64`) leaves `window.scrollY === 0`, and the bottom edge of `[data-testid="subnet-tool"]` is at or above `window.innerHeight` (650). At that point the island holds the CIDR row, the hero and all seven IPv6 field cards, each with its copy button (a /64 renders no subdivision section and no boundary note). This is the same full-output no-scroll target as the IPv4 default. `tests/e2e/subnet-layout.spec.ts` measures and enforces it (Task 2). At 1280x600 the IPv6 result is logged but not gated.

Planned headroom (planning-time simulation of the target layout on the live dev page): subnet-tool bottom about 528px for both /64 samples at 1366x650, about 122px inside the viewport.
