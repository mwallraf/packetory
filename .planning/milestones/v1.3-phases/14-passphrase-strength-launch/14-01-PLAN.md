| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-14-01 | Information Disclosure | word-list delivery | medium | mitigate | Literal dynamic import only, never prefetched; prod spec (a)/(b) scans every JS chunk body for the EFF marker before and after the toggle (Task 2; prohibition) |
